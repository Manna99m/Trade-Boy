Hero Wars Asgard: How to Deal the Maximum 1.72 Billion Damage to Osh in One Battle

Published: October 2026 | By: Trade Boy

Want to deal the maximum possible damage to Osh in a single Asgard battle in Hero Wars Dominion Era? With the right team, carefully selected buffs, precise manual timing, and a little luck, you can reach the battle's damage limit of 1.72 billion damage.

In this guide, I'll explain the exact team composition, the buffs you need, how to execute the strategy step by step, and the hero upgrade restrictions that are essential for making this setup work.

I'll also explain why dealing more damage in a single battle is impossible under these conditions, even if you have time remaining on the timer.

The Best Team for Maximum Osh Damage

Understanding the Maximum Damage Limit

Before discussing the battle strategy, it's important to understand how the Osh encounter works.

A standard Asgard battle has a two-minute timer. However, Osh battles have an additional restriction: once you defeat Osh twice during a single battle, the encounter ends immediately, even if time remains on the clock.

Osh's maximum level is 160, and at that level, the boss has exactly 860,079,181 HP. To keep things simple, I'll round that to 860 million in the rest of this guide.

This gives us the following calculation:

  • Osh's HP at level 160: 860,079,181
  • Damage from the first kill: 860,079,181
  • Damage from the second kill: 860,079,181
Maximum damage = 860,079,181 × 2 = 1,720,158,362 ≈ 1.72 billion

Therefore, the maximum possible damage in one battle against a full-health, level-160 Osh is 1.72 billion damage (1,720,158,362 exactly), assuming you defeat the boss twice.

How to Deal 1.72 Billion Damage: Step-by-Step Strategy

The most important part of this strategy is knowing when to activate your heroes' abilities.

You need to obtain the correct artifact bonuses before using Cornelius' ultimate. Ideally, you want to secure four Armor Penetration artifact bonuses, one Critical Hit bonus, and one Physical Attack bonus.

During the battle, you'll have approximately four opportunities to set up the required bonuses. You only need to execute the combination successfully twice to reach the maximum damage.

Follow these steps carefully.

Step 1: Start the Battle Without Using Hero Skills

At the beginning of the battle, don't activate any hero skills immediately.

Instead, repeatedly use Isaac's ultimate until all the Defense Spheres are destroyed.

Destroying these spheres is an important part of preparing the battle. Continue using Isaac's ultimate and avoid activating the other heroes' abilities too early.

Once all the Defense Spheres have been destroyed, wait for the next phase.

Step 2: Watch for the Meteor Phase

After the Defense Spheres are destroyed, pay attention to the falling meteors.

This is the moment when you need to start preparing your damage combination.

As soon as the meteors begin falling, activate:

  • Sebastian's ultimate
  • Nebula's ultimate

The purpose is to obtain the Critical Hit and Physical Attack artifact bonuses required for the setup.

Timing matters here. You want to coordinate your abilities so that the necessary bonuses are available before using Cornelius' ultimate.

Step 3: Keep Your Heroes Alive During the Meteors

The meteor phase can put your team at risk, so you must monitor your heroes' health.

If any hero's health drops below approximately 40% and they are in danger of dying, activate Martha's ultimate immediately.

Martha's Armor artifact bonus can provide protection to your entire team, helping them survive the meteor damage.

Keeping your heroes alive is essential because losing a hero can disrupt the carefully planned sequence of abilities and bonuses.

Don't focus exclusively on damage during this phase. You also need to preserve the team so that you can complete the setup.

Step 4: Obtain Four Armor Penetration Artifact Bonuses

After activating Sebastian's and Nebula's ultimates, continue using Isaac's ultimate.

Your next objective is to obtain four Armor Penetration artifact bonuses.

These bonuses are a critical part of the strategy because they prepare Cornelius for the powerful ultimate attack that follows.

Keep monitoring the artifact activations and wait until all four Armor Penetration bonuses have appeared.

Do not rush into the next step before the required bonuses are ready. Using Cornelius' ultimate too early can prevent you from achieving the damage needed for a successful kill.

Step 5: Activate Cornelius' and Albus' Ultimates TogetherCornelius' Heavy Wisdom + Albus' ultimate

Once you have obtained all four Armor Penetration artifact bonuses, activate Cornelius' ultimate and Albus' ultimate together.

This is the most important moment in the entire strategy.

The combination relies on several damage-enhancing mechanics working together. When the required bonuses are active and the timing is correct, Cornelius can deal an enormous amount of damage to Osh.

However, there is also an element of luck involved. For the strategy to work as intended, Cornelius' ultimate needs to land a critical hit.

If the critical hit doesn't occur at the right moment, you may fail to deal enough damage to finish the boss in that attempt.

This is why manual gameplay is essential. You need to watch the battle, wait for the correct bonuses, and activate the abilities at the right time.

Why Sebastian and Albus Are Essential

The damage combination becomes even more powerful because of Sebastian's skills and Albus' abilities.

Several mechanics work together to amplify the final damage.

Sebastian's Battle SongSecond skill, with Striking Solo

When the required conditions are met, Sebastian's third skill can add 134% extra pure damage to the affected heroes.

This additional pure damage is a major part of the strategy because it increases the damage output beyond what the heroes would otherwise deal.

The timing and conditions of the skill interaction are important, so make sure the necessary effects are active when executing the combination.

Albus' Violet SkillPure Victory

Albus is assigned to Sebastian as his patron pet.

Albus' violet skill increases the pure damage dealt by his master by 75%.

This makes Albus especially valuable for the strategy because Sebastian's bonus pure damage becomes even more effective.

The combination of Sebastian's additional pure damage and Albus' amplification contributes significantly to the enormous damage output required for the Osh kill.

Albus' UltimateBreath of the Ancients

Albus' ultimate adds another major source of damage to the combination.

When activated at the correct moment, Albus can deal more than 200% of the damage Sebastian has just dealt, according to the interaction used in this strategy.

This extra damage is what helps complete the combination and deliver enough damage to defeat Osh.

The key is to coordinate the effects correctly rather than activating abilities randomly. If the setup works as intended, the combined damage can be sufficient to eliminate the boss.

How to Secure the Second Osh Kill

Defeating Osh once is only half the objective. To reach the maximum possible damage, you must repeat the successful damage combination for the second kill.

The second attempt follows essentially the same process as the first.

  1. Wait for the meteors to begin falling.
  2. Activate Sebastian's and Nebula's ultimates at the appropriate moment.
  3. Obtain the required Critical Hit and Physical Attack artifact bonuses.
  4. Continue using Isaac's ultimate until you obtain four Armor Penetration artifact bonuses.
  5. Activate Cornelius' and Albus' ultimates together when the setup is ready.
  6. Aim for another successful critical hit and complete the second kill.

Remember that the battle ends immediately after the second Osh kill.

You have approximately four opportunities during the battle to establish the required artifact bonuses, but you only need to execute the full combination successfully twice.

Because the strategy depends on precise timing and critical hits, the result isn't guaranteed on every attempt. You may need to repeat the battle until the necessary conditions align.

The key to reaching 1.72 billion damage is executing the full combination twice, not simply activating every ultimate as soon as possible.

Hero Upgrade Restrictions: Important Details

One of the most important aspects of this strategy is the way Isaac and Cornelius are upgraded.

If you upgrade these heroes without considering their specific requirements, the strategy may no longer work as intended.

Pay particular attention to Isaac's first artifact and Cornelius' skills and artifact.

Isaac: Keep His First Artifact at Level 114

Isaac's first artifact must remain at level 114.

Do not over-upgrade it for this strategy. Increasing its level too much can interfere with the required mechanics and prevent the team from performing as intended.

You should also keep Isaac's Magic Defense and Armor Penetration stats as low as possible.

However, his health is different: higher health is preferable.

Use the following upgrade guidelines for Isaac:

These restrictions are intentional. The goal is not to make Isaac as powerful as possible in every respect, but to preserve the specific setup required for this damage strategy.

Cornelius: Upgrade His First Skill to Level 130

Cornelius also has specific upgrade requirements.

For this strategy:

  • Upgrade his first skill to level 130.
  • Keep his other skills at level 1.
  • Do not upgrade his first artifact.
  • Ideally, do not unlock his first artifact at all.

Keeping Cornelius' other skills at level 1 is not absolutely essential, but it can still be useful for maintaining the intended setup.

The most important artifact restriction is to avoid upgrading or unlocking his first artifact, as specified for this strategy.

These requirements may seem unusual if you're accustomed to maximizing every hero's upgrades. However, this is a specialized setup designed around particular battle mechanics rather than a conventional progression build.

What About the Other Heroes?

The remaining heroes do not require the same strict upgrade limitations for this strategy.

You can generally upgrade Nebula, Sebastian, and Martha without significantly affecting the core setup described in this guide.

Nevertheless, keep in mind that the overall performance still depends on your team composition, battle timing, and the interaction between the required skills and artifact bonuses.
