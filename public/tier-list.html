Hero Wars Tier List 2026 – All 75 Heroes Ranked from S Tier to D Tier

By Trade Boy • Updated for 2026 Meta • Hero Wars Dominion Era • 75 Heroes Ranked

Choosing the right heroes to upgrade is one of the most important decisions in Hero Wars Dominion Era. With so many heroes available, knowing which ones are worth investing in can save you a massive amount of resources and help you build significantly stronger teams.

In this guide, I'm going to rank all 75 heroes in Hero Wars Dominion Era, from S tier down to D tier.

This tier list is based on my in-depth experience with the game and the current 2026 meta, considering every major game mode: Arena, Grand Arena, Guild War, Clash of Worlds, and Asgard (Osh & Maestro).

Some heroes are extremely powerful in specific situations, while others are universally useful in almost every game mode. That's why I'm considering their overall usefulness rather than judging them based on just one team combination.

Whether you're a beginner or an experienced player, this guide will help you understand which heroes are worth upgrading, which heroes have strong team synergies, and which heroes you probably shouldn't prioritize.

A Tier
Verdict: My ranking: A Tier.

Adam is a really strong hero, and one of his most interesting abilities is his ability to dodge different types of incoming damage.

Unlike most dodge-based heroes, who primarily dodge physical attacks, Adam can avoid different types of damage. Heidi has a similar mechanic, but Adam's ability makes him particularly interesting.

He can be a very difficult hero to deal with when placed in the right team composition.

He's definitely worth considering if you're looking for a hero with strong defensive capabilities.

💡 Final Thoughts – Which Hero Should You Upgrade?

Now that we've covered all 75 heroes, let's look at the most important takeaways from this tier list.

If you're looking for the strongest overall investments, pay particular attention to the S-tier heroes:

  • Fluffy: Currently dominating multiple game modes, especially when paired with Electra.
  • Isaac: One of the best counters against magic teams and completely irreplaceable in Asgard (Osh & Maestro).
  • Nebula: An incredibly flexible support hero who amplifies the damage output of physical and magic teammates alike.
  • Sebastian: Provides essential protection against control debuffs and delivers massive critical-hit scaling.

For A-tier heroes, Adam, Augustus, Lara Croft, Lyria, Morrigan, and Orion are all exceptional choices depending on the core team archetype you build (Dodge, Undead, or Magic Burst).

B-tier heroes are also well worth upgrading when you have a specific combination in mind. However, I wouldn't recommend upgrading every hero just because they're ranked highly. Building a focused 5-hero core with airtight synergy is always more effective than spreading resources thin across individually powerful heroes.

Finally, don't completely disregard C and D-tier heroes. Certain heroes like Rufus, Cornelius, Mojo, and Jhu provide unmatched utility in specific PvE boss encounters or niche PvP counter situations.

Conclusion

This completes my comprehensive Hero Wars Dominion Era Tier List, covering all 75 heroes from Adam to Ziri.

These rankings represent my personal opinion based on the current 2026 meta and extensive testing across all game modes. Consider this tier list an actionable guide to make smarter investment decisions rather than an unbendable rulebook.
