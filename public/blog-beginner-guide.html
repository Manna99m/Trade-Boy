The Ultimate Beginner's Guide to Hero Wars (2026)

Published: October 2026 | By: Trade Boy Admin

Hero Wars is a game of resource management disguised as an RPG. The number one mistake new players make is trying to level up every hero they unlock. In this comprehensive guide, we'll explain exactly what you need to focus on during your first three months of playing.

1. The "Top 5" Rule

You only need 5 heroes for the Arena, the Campaign, and most of the Tower. Your resources (Gold, EXP potions, Runes, and Sparks of Power) are severely limited. If you spread them across 15 different heroes, you will end up with a weak roster that gets crushed by focused teams.

Pick one tank, one healer, one main damage dealer (DPS), and two support heroes. Pour 90% of your resources into your Tank and your DPS. Your healer and supports only need enough health to survive area-of-effect (AoE) damage.

2. Managing Your Emeralds

Emeralds are the premium currency in Hero Wars. Do not spend them on opening heroic chests unless there is a specific 55% discount event. The absolute best way to spend Emeralds is on Energy. Buying the 50-emerald energy refills every day allows you to farm more gear, get more player experience, and level up faster than your peers.

3. Joining an Active Guild

You cannot play this game solo. The moment you reach level 30, you need to find an active guild in the Silver or Gold league. Why? Because of Titan spheres and Guild War trophies. These resources are locked behind guild activity.

  • Titan Potions: You need thousands of these to level your titans.
  • Silver/Gold Trophies: Used to buy exclusive hero soul stones and champion skins.
  • Activity Rewards: Active guilds unlock daily runes and spheres for all members.

4. The Tower is Your Gold Mine

The Tower is the most important daily activity for your economy. It provides millions of gold. The trick to the Tower is that enemy levels scale with your Player Level, not your Hero Level. If your Player Level is 60, but your top 5 heroes are only level 45 because you didn't have enough EXP potions, the Tower will be impossible to clear.

Always keep your top 5 heroes exactly equal to your Player Level. If you can't afford to level them, stop claiming daily quests that give Player EXP until you catch up.

5. Conclusion

Hero Wars is a marathon, not a sprint. Log in daily, complete your quests, focus on a core team of 5 heroes, and never waste your emeralds. If you need help picking those 5 heroes, be sure to check out our Hero Tier List to see who is currently dominating the meta!
