Conquer the Triple Draft

The Triple Draft is the ultimate test of depth and versatility in your Hero Wars roster. Designed for Grand Arena enthusiasts and top-tier Guild War strategists, this format requires you to draft three completely distinct, fully functional teams. You must balance your resources—if you put all your best heroes into one team, your other two will suffer.

Success in the Triple Draft comes down to understanding resource allocation and recognizing niche synergies. You need to know which heroes can function independently and which require specific enablers. Can your magic team survive without a dedicated magic penetration buffer? Does your physical team have enough armor penetration to punch through the enemy tank?

By simulating Triple Drafts with our tool, you will learn how to build cohesive primary, secondary, and tertiary teams. You'll master the art of the 'hidden pick' and learn how to bait your opponent into over-committing to counter a strategy you aren't even relying on. Elevate your Grand Arena performance today!
