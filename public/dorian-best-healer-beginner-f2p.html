Dorian: Is He the Best Healer for Beginners and F2P Players in Hero Wars?

Published: November 2026 By: Trade Boy Hero Wars: Dominion Era F2P Strategy Est. Reading Time: 6 mins

Are you looking for a powerful healer in Hero Wars: Dominion Era but don't have enough resources to invest in multiple expensive heroes?

Then Dorian might be exactly what you need!

Unlike traditional healers who mainly restore health through their active abilities, Dorian offers something completely different. His unique fourth skill, Initiation, provides a Vampirism Aura that allows nearby allies to recover health while dealing damage to enemies.

Even better, this ability doesn't depend on Dorian having high Magic Attack, expensive equipment, or heavily upgraded skins. The most important thing is upgrading his fourth skill.

This makes Dorian an excellent option for beginners and Free-to-Play (F2P) players who want to build a sustainable team without spreading their precious resources too thin.

In this guide, we'll explain why Dorian is worth investing in, how his Vampirism Aura works, which heroes benefit from it, and how to upgrade him efficiently.

1. Why Is Dorian So Good for Beginners and F2P Players?

One of the biggest problems for new players in Hero Wars is keeping their heroes alive.

You might have a powerful damage dealer, but that hero cannot do much if they get defeated before dealing enough damage.

This is where Dorian becomes extremely useful.

Instead of relying entirely on direct healing, Dorian gives nearby teammates the ability to restore their own health through the damage they deal.

This delivers several major advantages:

  • Less dependence on traditional healing: Your damage dealers can recover health continuously while attacking enemies.
  • Works with different damage types: Both physical, magic, and pure damage dealers benefit from his Vampirism Aura.
  • Passive support: His most important ability doesn't require manual activation, ultimate energy, or specific timing.
  • Low stat investment: The strength of his Vampirism Aura depends strictly on skill level, not Dorian's Magic Attack or Physical Attack stats.
  • Continues working after his defeat: His fourth skill remains active on the battlefield even when Dorian dies.

F2P Resource Reality: For Free-to-Play players, resource management is everything. You cannot upgrade every hero, skin, artifact, glyph, and piece of equipment at the same time. Dorian lets you invest in a support hero without needing to maximize every aspect of his development just to obtain his most important benefit.

That is what makes him such an interesting choice for a beginner account.

2. Dorian's Fourth Skill: Initiation – His Biggest Strength

Violet Skill • Passive Vampirism Aura

Initiation (Max Level: 115% Vampirism)

Emits an aura that grants massive Vampirism to nearby allies, allowing them to heal proportional to damage dealt.

Let's talk about the most important part of this guide.

Dorian's fourth skill is called Initiation.

This passive ability creates a Vampirism Aura around Dorian, increasing the vampirism of nearby allies.

At maximum skill level, the aura provides 115% Vampirism.

What exactly is Vampirism?

Vampirism allows a hero to recover health based on the damage they deal to enemies.

For example, if a hero has 10% Vampirism and deals 1,000 damage, they recover 100 health from that damage.

Now imagine giving your damage dealers an additional source of vampirism through Dorian (up to 115%!).

Instead of depending entirely on a healer to restore their health, they can recover health while continuing to attack. This is particularly helpful for heroes who deal consistent damage throughout a battle.

And because Initiation is a passive ability, you don't need to wait for Dorian's ultimate or depend on a particular skill rotation. As long as your heroes are within the aura, they can benefit from it.

Why Is This Skill So Good for F2P Players?

Here's another important reason why I recommend Dorian.

His fourth skill does not require high Magic Attack, Physical Attack, or expensive equipment to provide its Vampirism Aura.

Its strength is determined solely by the skill level.

This means you can get the main benefit of Dorian without spending enormous resources on his offensive stats.

You still need to keep his hero level and skill level competitive, but you don't need to build him like a primary damage dealer. For a beginner, this is a significant advantage.

You can focus your valuable equipment, skins, and other major investments on your main damage dealers and tank while keeping Dorian's most important ability upgraded.

3. Important: Dorian's Aura Does Not Affect the Entire Team!

This is probably the most important mechanic you need to understand before using Dorian.

His Vampirism Aura has a specific range. That means not every hero in your five-hero team will automatically receive the bonus.

Only nearby allies inside the aura benefit from Initiation.

💡 How to tell which heroes are receiving the bonus:

During a battle, look underneath your heroes' feet. Heroes affected by Dorian's Vampirism Aura will have a glowing circle underneath them. If a hero doesn't have this glowing circle, that hero is outside Dorian's aura and isn't receiving the additional vampirism.

This is especially important when choosing your team. You might have five powerful heroes, but if your main damage dealer is outside Dorian's aura, you're not getting the full value of his most important ability.

Does the Aura Work in Every Game Mode?

Not necessarily on exactly the same heroes.

Different game modes can have different battlefield sizes and formations. Because Dorian's aura has a fixed range, a hero who receives the bonus in one game mode might be outside the aura in another.

So, don't assume that your team positioning will work identically everywhere. Always check the glowing circles during actual battles.

4. Dorian's Biggest Advantage: His Aura Still Works After He Dies!

Here's something that makes Dorian even more interesting.

What happens when Dorian gets defeated during a battle?

Normally, when you lose your healer, your team loses an important source of support.

But Dorian is different.

His fourth skill, Initiation, continues working even after his defeat!

Yes, even when Dorian is no longer alive on the battlefield, his Vampirism Aura remains active with the same range.

This means your surviving damage dealers can continue receiving the vampirism bonus and recovering health while attacking enemies.

Tactical Ambush: Imagine your opponent defeats Dorian early, expecting your team to crumble without healing support. However, your main damage dealers are still inside his aura. They continue recovering massive chunks of health through their attacks, turning what looked like a defeat into a clutch victory.

For beginners, this is another reason why Dorian can be a valuable long-term investment. His most important contribution doesn't immediately disappear when he gets defeated.

Of course, this doesn't mean you should ignore Dorian's survivability completely. Keeping him alive allows him to contribute through his other abilities as well. But you don't have to panic if he falls during a battle.

5. Best Heroes to Use With Dorian

Now that you understand how his Vampirism Aura works, let's discuss which heroes can benefit the most from him.

The best partners are generally damage dealers who stay within Dorian's aura and can deal consistent damage.

Orion

Magic Damage Dealer

Orion is one of the most famous partners for Dorian. He specializes in rapid-fire magic missiles and charges energy constantly. With Dorian's Vampirism Aura, Orion recovers huge amounts of health with every rocket barrage, making him surprisingly durable.

Heidi

Pure Damage Dealer

Heidi deals pure damage that bypasses enemy armor and magic defense entirely. Dorian provides Heidi with continuous self-sustain while Heidi wears down the opponent's entire lineup. Dorian's aura works equally well on pure damage!

Lian

Magic Damage & Control

Lian features potent charm control and steady magic damage. When placed next to Dorian, she gains the survivability needed to drag fights out and lock down opponents over long, grueling battles.

Elmir

Physical Damage Dealer

Elmir's high physical burst and clone-jumping mechanics create exciting synergy with Dorian. As Elmir leaps back into Dorian's aura, his physical damage rapidly replenishes his health pool.

How to Choose the Right Teammates

You don't have to use all these heroes together. The important thing is to understand what makes a good Dorian partner.

Look for heroes who:

  • Deal consistent, repeated damage.
  • Can remain physically positioned inside Dorian's Vampirism Aura.
  • Benefit from additional sustain rather than pure burst protection.
  • Have useful synergy with your main tank and other support heroes.

Remember, Dorian is a support hero, not a complete team by himself. You still need a strong tank, damage dealers, and other useful support abilities depending on your opponent.

6. Dorian Upgrade Guide: What Should F2P Players Upgrade First?

If you're a beginner, you should be very careful about how you spend your resources.

There is no reason to invest equally in every ability of every hero.

For Dorian, my main recommendation is simple:

Golden Rule: Prioritize his fourth skill, Initiation. This is the ability that makes him special, and it's the main reason we're investing in him.

Skill Upgrade Priority

Skill Priority Recommendation & Analysis
Initiation (4th)
Highest Keep it upgraded as high as possible. Unlocks at Violet promotion and delivers the game-changing 115% Vampirism Aura.
Fountain of Blood (1st)
Secondary Marks the enemy with the highest health; allies attacking the mark heal. Upgrade when resources allow.
Ancestors' Amulet (2nd)
Secondary Dorian sacrifices 20% of his current health to heal the lowest-health ally. Provides additional direct healing.
Wings of Night (3rd)
Lower Releases a flock of bats that attack enemies and return health to Dorian. Useful for Dorian's personal healing, but least crucial for the team.

Do You Need Expensive Skins and Artifacts?

No, not to get the main benefit of his Vampirism Aura.

This is another reason I like Dorian for F2P players.

His fourth skill's Vampirism Aura is based strictly on skill level rather than his Magic Attack, Physical Attack, or other hero stats.

So you don't need to spend all your resources trying to maximize his skins and artifacts before he becomes useful.

Of course, additional health and defensive stats can help him survive longer. If you have spare resources later, improving his survivability is worthwhile. But as a beginner, focus your valuable resources on your main team's overall development rather than spending heavily on Dorian's optional upgrades.

7. Dorian's Weaknesses: Is He Good for Every Team?

Although Dorian is an excellent support hero, he isn't perfect.

There are a few key limitations you should understand before making him your permanent healer:

⚠️ Limited Aura Range

His Vampirism Aura only affects nearby allies. Frontline tanks often stand outside the aura circle, meaning they won't receive the vampiric healing unless they step back into range.

⚠️ Dependent on Ally Damage

Dorian's healing comes from allies attacking. If your damage dealers get stunned, blinded, silenced, or frozen, they deal zero damage and therefore receive zero healing.

⚠️ Vulnerable to Backline Pressure

Dorian fights from the backline. Opponents like Cleaver, Jhu, Kayla, Jorgen, and Luther can pull, disrupt, or snipe backline heroes, complicating Dorian's positioning.

⚠️ Not a Universal Healer

No single healer fits every battle. Teams that rely on defensive stall without active damage output may need burst healers like Martha, Thea, or Celeste instead.

8. Final Verdict: Should F2P Players Invest in Dorian?

🏆 My Recommendation: A Resounding YES!

Dorian is one of the most interesting healer and support heroes to consider for a beginner or F2P account.

His fourth skill, Initiation, provides something that many traditional healers cannot offer: the ability for nearby allies to heal themselves simply by attacking.

You don't need to heavily invest in his offensive stats to benefit from this ability. You mainly need to keep his fourth skill upgraded, build a team around his aura, and ensure your key damage dealers stay within its range.

And don't forget the biggest advantage: his Vampirism Aura continues working even after Dorian is defeated.

For a beginner trying to get maximum value from limited resources, that's an exceptionally attractive feature.

Is he the best healer for absolutely every team? No. But if you have compatible damage dealers and want a support hero who boosts team survivability without requiring a massive stat investment, Dorian deserves a spot on your upgrade list.

My advice is to unlock him when possible, prioritize Initiation to max level, and experiment with different teammates in Training battles!
