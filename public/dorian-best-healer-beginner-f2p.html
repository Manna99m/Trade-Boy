Dorian: The Best Healer for Beginners and F2P Players in Hero Wars?

Published: November 2026 By: Trade Boy Hero Wars: Dominion Era Resource Management F2P Strategy Est. Reading Time: 6 mins

Starting your journey in Hero Wars: Dominion Era can be challenging, especially when it comes to managing resources.

As a beginner, you have limited gold, skill points, equipment, skin stones, artifact resources, and many other materials. At the same time, you need to develop your tank, damage dealers, and support heroes to build a strong team.

And here's the biggest problem: you simply cannot afford to invest heavily in too many heroes at the same time.

This is where Dorian becomes extremely interesting.

Unlike many traditional healers who may require considerable investment to provide reliable healing and support, Dorian can offer his most valuable benefit with relatively low investment.

His fourth skill, Initiation, provides a powerful Vampirism Aura that allows nearby allies to recover health while dealing damage.

You don't need to maximize all his skins, artifacts, glyphs, or offensive stats to take advantage of this ability.

Instead, you can focus on upgrading his most important skill and spend your remaining resources developing your main damage dealers and tank.

This makes Dorian one of the most resource-efficient healer options for beginners and Free-to-Play (F2P) players.

In this guide, we'll explain why Dorian is worth investing in, how his Vampirism Aura works, which heroes benefit from it, and how to get the most out of him without wasting your valuable resources.

1. Why Dorian Is an Excellent Choice for Beginners and F2P Players

One of the biggest mistakes beginners make in Hero Wars is trying to develop too many heroes simultaneously.

You might find an interesting tank, a powerful damage dealer, another support hero, and a healer. Naturally, you want to upgrade all of them.

But resources are limited.

If you spread your resources across too many heroes, your entire team can end up underdeveloped.

You need a strong tank to absorb damage, a reliable damage dealer to defeat opponents, and support heroes who can keep everyone alive.

However, traditional healers can sometimes require additional investment to perform effectively.

Dorian offers a different approach.

His biggest strength comes from his fourth skill, Initiation, which provides Vampirism to nearby allies.

This means your damage dealers can recover health through the damage they deal instead of depending entirely on Dorian's direct healing abilities.

And here's why this matters so much for beginners:

  • Less investment in your healer: You don't need to maximize Dorian's offensive stats to benefit from his main ability.
  • More resources for your main heroes: You can prioritize your tank and primary damage dealers instead of heavily developing another hero.
  • Works with different damage dealers: Both physical, magic, and pure damage dealers can benefit from his Vampirism Aura.
  • Passive healing support: His fourth skill doesn't require manual activation.
  • Continues working after his defeat: His aura remains active even when Dorian dies.

Think about it this way.

Instead of spending a large portion of your resources trying to make your healer stronger, you can develop your main damage dealers and let Dorian provide additional survivability through his passive ability.

That's a much more attractive investment strategy for an account with limited resources.

Of course, you still need to level Dorian and upgrade his important skill. But compared with heavily developing multiple heroes, his low-investment approach can help you manage your resources much more efficiently.

2. Dorian's Fourth Skill: Initiation – The Secret Behind His Low-Investment Value

The main reason Dorian is so valuable is his fourth skill, Initiation.

Fourth Skill • Passive Vampirism Aura • Violet Promotion

Initiation (Max Level: 115% Vampirism)

Creates a persistent Vampirism Aura around Dorian. Nearby allies heal for a percentage of the damage they inflict.

This passive ability creates a Vampirism Aura around Dorian, increasing the vampirism of nearby allies.

At maximum skill level, the aura provides 115% Vampirism.

But what does this actually mean?

Vampirism allows heroes to recover health based on the damage they deal to enemies.

For example, if a hero has 10% Vampirism and deals 1,000 damage, they recover 100 health from that damage.

Dorian provides an additional source of vampirism to nearby teammates, allowing them to recover more health while attacking.

Imagine having a damage dealer who can attack enemies continuously but doesn't have any reliable self-healing.

Normally, that hero would depend on your healer to survive.

With Dorian, things work differently.

As long as the hero is inside Dorian's aura, they can recover health while dealing damage. The more damage they deal, the more health they can potentially recover. And this works with both physical and magic damage dealers.

Why Does This Require Less Investment?

Here's the most important part for F2P players.

The strength of Dorian's fourth skill depends on its skill level, not his Magic Attack or Physical Attack.

You don't need to spend enormous resources upgrading his offensive stats to make Initiation useful.

You can prioritize the fourth skill while keeping his other upgrades relatively modest.

This doesn't mean Dorian requires absolutely no investment. You still need to develop his hero level, unlock his fourth skill, and keep it upgraded.

But you don't need to maximize every aspect of his development before he can provide his main benefit.

That's what makes him such an attractive option for beginners.

F2P Core Strategy: Invest in the skill that matters, then invest your remaining resources in your main team.

3. Important: Dorian's Vampirism Aura Has Limited Range

Although Dorian's aura is extremely useful, there's one important mechanic you need to understand.

His Vampirism Aura doesn't automatically affect all five heroes.

It has a specific range around Dorian, meaning only nearby allies receive the bonus.

So, depending on your team formation and game mode, some heroes might benefit from the aura while others don't.

How can you identify the affected heroes?

It's actually very simple.

During battle, look underneath your heroes' feet.

💡 Battlefield Indicator

Heroes receiving Dorian's Vampirism Aura will have a glowing circle underneath them. If a hero doesn't have this glowing circle, they're outside the aura and aren't receiving the additional vampirism.

This is extremely important when building your team.

You don't want to invest in a damage dealer specifically for Dorian's synergy only to discover that the hero isn't receiving his aura.

Always check the glowing circles during actual battles.

Also remember that battlefield formations and sizes can differ between game modes. A hero who receives the bonus in one mode might not receive it in another.

4. Dorian's Aura Continues Working Even After He Dies!

Here's another reason why I really like Dorian.

What happens if your healer gets defeated during battle?

Normally, losing your healer can be a serious problem because your team loses an important source of support.

But Dorian is different.

His fourth skill, Initiation, continues working even after he dies!

Yes, even when Dorian is defeated, his Vampirism Aura remains active with the same range.

This means nearby surviving allies can continue receiving the vampirism bonus and recovering health while attacking enemies.

Imagine your opponent defeats Dorian early in the battle.

You might expect your team to lose its healing support, but your main damage dealers can continue benefiting from his aura.

This can make a significant difference in longer battles.

And for beginners, it's another reason why Dorian can provide excellent value without requiring enormous investment in his personal survivability.

Of course, keeping him alive is still beneficial because he can use his other abilities. But even if he falls, his most important contribution doesn't immediately disappear.

5. Best Heroes to Use With Dorian

Now that we understand how his aura works, let's discuss which heroes can benefit from Dorian.

The best partners are generally damage dealers who stay inside his aura and deal consistent damage.

Here are some interesting options:

Orion

Magic Damage Dealer

Orion is a magic damage dealer who can deal considerable damage throughout a battle. With Dorian's Vampirism Aura, he gains an additional source of healing while attacking. This can improve his survivability without requiring you to invest in another dedicated healer.

Heidi

Pure Damage Dealer

Heidi is another interesting option. He deals pure damage and can gradually wear down opponents. Dorian provides additional survivability while Heidi continues dealing damage, sustaining through prolonged clashes.

Augustus

Magic Damage Dealer

Augustus unleashes relentless waves of magic damage across the enemy team. When stationed inside Dorian's Vampirism Aura, every magic strike and area-of-effect blast triggers life-steal, giving Augustus massive self-healing and allowing him to sustain through heavy enemy counter-attacks.

Adam

Physical Damage & Marksman

Adam fights from the backline, making him a natural neighbor to Dorian in nearly every formation. His rapid high-velocity gunfire and critical hits deal immense physical damage, which Dorian's aura instantly converts into rapid-fire health recovery to keep Adam alive against backline snipers.

Iris

Pure Damage Dealer & Debuffer

Iris pulls the souls of enemy frontline tanks and unleashes devastating pure damage that bypasses all defenses. Because Dorian's Vampirism Aura applies to pure damage as well, Iris rapidly restores her own health bar with each soul strike, making her virtually unkillable over drawn-out battles.

Remember, these are potential synergies, not universal team recommendations.

The most important thing is checking whether your chosen hero actually receives Dorian's aura during battle.

You should also consider your tank, other support heroes, and the opponent's lineup when building your final team.

6. Dorian Upgrade Guide: Where Should You Spend Your Resources?

This is probably the most important section for F2P players.

If you're using Dorian because you want to save resources, you should have a clear upgrade priority.

My recommendation is simple:

Primary Commandment: Prioritize his fourth skill, Initiation. This is the ability that makes Dorian valuable, so keep it upgraded as much as your hero progression allows.

Skill Upgrade Priority

Skill Priority Recommendation
Initiation (4th)
Highest Keep it upgraded as much as possible. Delivers the core 115% Vampirism Aura.
Fountain of Blood (1st)
Secondary Upgrade when resources allow. Marks highest-health enemy for bonus team healing.
Ancestors' Amulet (2nd)
Secondary Provides additional direct healing by transferring Dorian's health to lowest-HP ally.
Wings of Night (3rd)
Lower Mainly helps Dorian's personal healing via bats. Keep lowest priority.

Initiation becomes available when Dorian reaches Violet promotion.

Once unlocked, make it your main priority.

Should You Upgrade Dorian's Skins?

You don't need to heavily invest in his skins just to benefit from his fourth skill.

Since Initiation's Vampirism Aura is determined by skill level, upgrading Dorian's offensive stats isn't necessary for providing that bonus.

Additional health and defensive stats can help him survive longer, but they shouldn't come before the development of your main heroes.

As an F2P player, you should be selective about where you spend your skin stones.

Your main tank and primary damage dealer will generally need much more attention.

What About Artifacts, Glyphs, and Equipment?

The same principle applies here.

Don't rush to maximize everything on Dorian.

Keep him sufficiently developed for your progression, prioritize his fourth skill, and spend your remaining resources where they provide the most value to your main team.

Remember, the objective isn't to make Dorian your strongest hero.

The objective is to use Dorian as an efficient support hero while developing the rest of your team.

7. Dorian's Weaknesses: What Should You Watch Out For?

Although Dorian is an excellent low-investment option, he isn't perfect.

There are a few limitations you should understand:

⚠️ Limited Aura Range

His biggest limitation is that only nearby allies receive his Vampirism Aura. Your frontline tank might not benefit from it.

⚠️ Depends on Damage

His main healing advantage comes from allies dealing damage. If your damage dealers are controlled or unable to attack, they won't recover much health through vampirism.

⚠️ Backline Vulnerability

Dorian can be targeted by opponents who specialize in attacking backline heroes (e.g. Cleaver, Jhu, Kayla, Jorgen, Luther). Certain enemy compositions can make it difficult for him to survive.

⚠️ Not a Universal Healer

Some teams need stronger direct healing or different support abilities. Dorian won't automatically solve every team's survivability problems.

However, these limitations don't remove his value. You simply need to understand how his aura works and choose compatible teammates.

8. Final Verdict: Is Dorian the Best Healer for Beginners and F2P Players?

In my opinion, Dorian is one of the best healer and support investments for beginners, especially if you're playing Free-to-Play.

The biggest challenge for a new account is resource management.

You cannot develop every hero simultaneously, and spending too much on secondary heroes can slow down the development of your main team.

Dorian offers a different approach.

Instead of heavily investing in his personal stats, you can focus on his fourth skill, Initiation, and let your damage dealers benefit from his Vampirism Aura.

This allows you to put more resources into the heroes that actually deal damage and protect your team.

And remember, his aura continues working even after he dies!

That's a very valuable mechanic, especially when you're trying to get the most out of a limited investment.

Is he the best healer for every team? No.

But if you're a beginner looking for a support hero who can provide excellent value without requiring massive investment, Dorian deserves serious consideration.

🏆 My Advice:

Unlock Dorian, prioritize his fourth skill, and invest your remaining resources in your main tank and damage dealers.

You don't always need the most expensive or heavily upgraded healer to build a strong team. Sometimes, choosing the right low-investment hero makes all the difference.
