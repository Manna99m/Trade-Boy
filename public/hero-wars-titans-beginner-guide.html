Hero Wars Titans Beginner Guide: Best Titans to Upgrade and Dungeon Strategy

Published: October 2026 | By: Trade Boy

If you've recently unlocked Titans at level 30 in Hero Wars Dominion Era, you might already be facing a frustrating problem: your Titans keep losing health, your Dungeon progress stops after a few floors, and you're not sure which Titans you should upgrade first.

Don't worry! Every player goes through this stage when starting their Titan journey.

The good news is that you don't need to upgrade every Titan equally to make progress. With the right investment strategy, proper Titan management, and a good understanding of the Dungeon, you can keep your Titans alive longer, progress deeper into the Dungeon, and work toward collecting 1,000 Titanite daily for your guild.

In this guide, we'll cover the six Titan elements, which elements beginners should prioritize, how Elemental and Neutral Doors work, why your main Titan tank is so important, and how to use your Water Titans and Water Totem more effectively.

Whether you're a completely new player or someone struggling to improve their daily Dungeon runs, this guide will help you build a stronger foundation for long-term Titan progression.

1. Understanding the Six Titan Elements

Hero Wars Dominion Era features six Titan elements:

  • Earth
  • Fire
  • Water
  • Light
  • Dark
  • Distortion

Each element has its own Titans and role in the broader Titan system. As you progress, you'll eventually want to develop Titans from different elements for different game modes.

However, beginners often make the mistake of spreading their resources across too many Titans at once.

Titan progression requires resources, and investing in every element equally can leave your entire roster underdeveloped. As a result, your Titans may struggle in the Dungeon, making it harder to earn the resources needed for future upgrades.

Instead, it's better to establish a clear priority.

My recommendation for beginners is to prioritize Water Titans while gradually developing your Earth and Fire Titans.

This approach gives you a foundation for Dungeon progression without completely neglecting the other elements you'll need later. Want to know which Titans are strongest? Check the Titans tier list.

2. Why Water Titans Should Be Your Priority

The Dungeon is one of the most important game modes for developing your Titans. It provides Titanite for your guild and offers opportunities to earn resources that help you strengthen your account.

Water Titans are particularly valuable for Dungeon progression because they can support a strategy built around the Water Totem and its healing capabilities.

The objective isn't simply to defeat the enemies in front of you. You also need to preserve your Titans' health so they can survive subsequent battles.

This is where a well-developed Water team becomes useful.

By investing in your Water Titans and Totem, you can build a more sustainable approach to Dungeon progression. Better health management can help you get through more battles before your team becomes too weak to continue.

However, this doesn't mean Water Titans can handle every Dungeon battle by themselves.

You'll also encounter elemental battles that require Titans from other elements. If your Earth and Fire Titans are too weak, your overall progress may eventually become limited.

That's why a balanced long-term plan is important.

The best investment approach for beginners

Rather than upgrading every Titan equally, follow this general priority:

  1. Water Titans: Establish your core team for Dungeon progression and your Water Totem strategy.
  2. Your main Titan tank: Invest enough to help your frontline survive and protect your other Titans.
  3. Earth Titans: Develop them so they can handle their required elemental battles.
  4. Fire Titans: Strengthen them as needed to improve your overall Dungeon progress.
  5. Other elements: Expand your investment as your account develops and your other game modes require it.

This is a general progression strategy rather than a strict upgrade order for every account. Your available Titans, current upgrades, and game mode requirements can affect which individual Titan deserves your next investment.

3. Why the Dungeon Is So Important

You might be wondering why this guide focuses so heavily on the Dungeon when Titans are also used in other game modes, including Guild War, Tournament of the Elements, and Clash of Worlds.

The reason is simple: the Dungeon helps you develop your Titan roster over time.

As you progress, you can earn Titanite for your guild and collect resources that support your account's development. Depending on the rewards available, these can include Gold, Titan EXP Potions, and Titan Soul Stones.

This creates a useful progression cycle.

Your Titans help you progress deeper into the Dungeon. Your Dungeon runs provide resources and Titanite. Those resources can then help you strengthen your Titans, making future Dungeon runs more manageable.

The stronger and more sustainable your team becomes, the better your chances of maintaining consistent progress.

That's why investing in your Dungeon team early can provide long-term value beyond the Dungeon itself.

Why 1,000 Titanite daily is a useful goal

For players who want to contribute more to their guild, collecting 1,000 Titanite daily can be an important target.

However, reaching this amount depends on your Titan development, available resources, Dungeon progression, and how effectively you manage your team.

If you're a beginner, don't worry if you can't reach this goal immediately. Focus on improving your roster and developing a consistent daily routine first.

As your Titans become stronger, you can gradually work toward higher Titanite milestones.

4. Dungeon Mechanics: Elemental Doors vs. Neutral Doors

One of the most important things to understand before developing your Dungeon strategy is the difference between Elemental Doors and Neutral Doors.

Knowing how these battles work will help you decide which Titans to use and when to preserve your weaker Titans.

Elemental Doors

Elemental Doors restrict your team to Titans of the selected element.

For example, in a Water elemental battle, you must use Water Titans. You cannot simply bring your strongest Fire or Earth Titans to handle the fight.

The same principle applies to the other elemental battles.

This is why focusing exclusively on Water Titans isn't a complete long-term solution. Even if your Water team is strong, your other elemental Titans still need enough development to survive the battles where they're required.

Beginner tip: Avoid spending all your resources on one element without considering the requirements of your other Dungeon battles.

Neutral Doors

Neutral Doors are different because they allow you to use Titans from different elements.

This flexibility makes them particularly important for managing your team's health.

Instead of being restricted to a single element, you can select Titans based on their current health, your available lineup, and the condition of your overall team.

For example, if some of your Earth or Fire Titans have lost a significant amount of health, a Neutral Door can provide an opportunity to bring in your Water Titans and use your Water Totem strategy.

Neutral Doors are therefore an important part of a plan built around preserving your Titans and progressing through more battles.

5. The Golden Rule: Prioritize Your Main Titan Tank

If there's one Titan investment principle every beginner should remember, it's this:

Don't neglect your main Titan tank.

Your tank is responsible for absorbing incoming damage and helping protect the rest of your team.

If your frontline is too weak, your other Titans may lose health more quickly. Even strong damage-dealing Titans can become ineffective if they cannot survive long enough to contribute.

This is why investing in your main tank can be more valuable than distributing your resources equally across every Titan.

A durable frontline gives your team a better chance of surviving battles and preserving the Titans you'll need for subsequent fights.

How to prioritize your tank

When deciding where to spend your resources, consider the following:

  • Is your main tank surviving the battles you currently face?
  • Are your other Titans losing too much health because your frontline is too weak?
  • Would strengthening your tank help you complete more Dungeon battles?
  • Are you spreading resources across too many Titans before establishing a reliable core team?

You don't necessarily need to max out your tank immediately. The goal is to invest enough to keep your frontline effective at your current stage of progression.

As your account grows, continue adjusting your investments according to your team's needs.

6. How to Keep Your Titans Alive in the Dungeon

Having strong Titans is important, but how you use them also matters.

One of the biggest mistakes beginners make is relying on auto battles without paying attention to the health of individual Titans.

When you're trying to progress deeper into the Dungeon, simply winning the current battle isn't always enough. You also need to consider whether your Titans will be healthy enough for the next fight.

That's why I recommend playing manually whenever possible.

Manual control gives you more opportunities to manage your abilities and make decisions based on the condition of your team.

Here are the main principles to follow.

Step 1: Monitor your Earth and Fire Titans

Pay close attention to your Earth and Fire Titans' health during their elemental battles.

If one of these Titans loses a significant amount of health, try to preserve it rather than continuing to use it unnecessarily.

When the available Dungeon route allows you to reach a Neutral Door, you may have an opportunity to switch your lineup and use your Titans more strategically.

The goal is to avoid exhausting the same Titans in every battle when you have other options available.

Step 2: Rotate your Titans when possible

Don't automatically use the same lineup in every battle.

Consider which Titans are healthy, which ones have already taken significant damage, and which elements are required for the next fight.

If your available Titans allow it, rotate your lineup to preserve weaker members of your team.

This is particularly important when you're trying to progress through multiple battles without losing your core Titans.

Step 3: Use Neutral Doors strategically

Neutral Doors give you more flexibility because you can select Titans from different elements.

When you reach one, assess the condition of your roster before choosing your lineup.

This is where a Water-focused strategy can become useful.

You can bring three Water Titans alongside two Titans from Earth or Fire, depending on which Titans need support and which ones you can safely use.

The idea is to take advantage of the flexibility of Neutral Doors while keeping your other elemental Titans available for the battles where they're needed.

Step 4: Play manually and manage your abilities

During a manual battle, pay attention to your Titans' health and the timing of their abilities.

Don't focus only on defeating the enemy as quickly as possible. You also want to finish the fight in a condition that allows your team to continue progressing.

Use your abilities thoughtfully, monitor the battle, and avoid unnecessary risks when possible.

With experience, you'll become better at recognizing when to use your strongest Titans and when to preserve them for later battles.

7. The Water Totem: An Important Part of Your Strategy

Your Water Totem is another important part of this Dungeon strategy.

Its healing capabilities can help restore your Titans' health, making it easier to maintain your team during longer Dungeon sessions.

This is one of the reasons I recommend prioritizing your Water team rather than spreading all your resources equally across every element.

When you reach a Neutral Door, bringing three Water Titans can help you activate your Water Totem, allowing you to take advantage of its healing.

However, the effectiveness of this approach depends on your Totem's development and the circumstances of the battle.

A weak Water Totem won't necessarily provide enough healing to compensate for a team that takes excessive damage. You still need to develop your Titans and manage your battles carefully.

How to get more value from your Water Totem

  • Develop your core Water Titans.
  • Improve your Water Totem as your resources allow.
  • Use Neutral Doors to take advantage of your flexible team selection.
  • Monitor your Titans' health instead of relying entirely on automatic battles.
  • Preserve weakened Earth and Fire Titans whenever your available route permits.

Remember that the objective is to create a sustainable progression strategy, not to depend on one ability to solve every problem.

8. How to Build a Sustainable Daily Dungeon Routine

Dungeon progression can take time, particularly when you're trying to maximize your daily Titanite collection.

You may not immediately reach 1,000 Titanite every day, and that's completely normal for a developing account.

Instead of pushing your Titans beyond their limits, focus on building a routine that gradually improves your progress.

Here's a simple approach you can follow.

Before entering the Dungeon

Review your Titan roster and identify which Titans are strongest, which ones need more investment, and whether your main tank can survive the battles you're currently facing.

During elemental battles

Use the required elemental Titans and monitor their health. Avoid unnecessary damage whenever possible.

When you reach a Neutral Door

Take advantage of the freedom to select Titans from different elements. Consider whether your Water Totem strategy can help restore health and whether weakened Titans should be preserved.

After your Dungeon session

Think about which part of your team struggled the most. If your tank consistently loses too much health, consider prioritizing its development. If your Water team or Totem is underdeveloped, focus on improving those areas.

Over time, this approach can help you make better use of your resources and develop a more reliable Dungeon team.

9. How Your Dungeon Progress Helps Your Guild

Your Dungeon progress doesn't benefit only your personal account.

Collecting Titanite also contributes to your guild's progress and helps your guild work toward its reward milestones.

By developing your Titans and improving your daily Dungeon performance, you can contribute more consistently to your guild.

This is especially important for players who want to support their guild's long-term development.

Even if you can't reach 1,000 Titanite immediately, every improvement to your Dungeon strategy can help you become a more consistent contributor.

As your roster becomes stronger, you can gradually increase your daily Titanite collection and work toward higher milestones.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Titan element should beginners upgrade first?

For a Dungeon-focused progression strategy, I recommend prioritizing Water Titans while gradually developing Earth and Fire Titans. This gives you a useful foundation without neglecting the other elements required for elemental battles.

Should I upgrade all my Titans equally?

No. Spreading resources too thinly can leave your entire roster underdeveloped. Focus on your core Titans first, especially your main tank and the Water Titans that support your Dungeon strategy.

What is the difference between Elemental Doors and Neutral Doors?

Elemental Doors restrict your team to Titans of the selected element. Neutral Doors allow you to use Titans from different elements, giving you more flexibility when choosing your lineup.

Why is my Titan tank so important?

Your tank helps absorb incoming damage and protect your other Titans. A stronger frontline can improve your team's ability to survive battles and maintain Dungeon progression.

Why should I play the Dungeon manually?

Manual play gives you more control over your abilities and allows you to make decisions based on your Titans' health. This can help you avoid unnecessary damage and preserve your team for subsequent battles.

How can I work toward 1,000 Titanite daily?

Focus on developing your core Titans, strengthening your main tank and Water Totem, learning the Dungeon mechanics, and managing your Titans' health carefully. Your results will depend on your account's development and how far you can progress.

Is focusing on Water Titans enough for the entire game?

No. Water Titans can be valuable for a Dungeon-focused strategy, but you'll also need to develop other elements for elemental battles and other game modes as your account progresses.

Final Thoughts

Titan progression in Hero Wars Dominion Era becomes much easier when you understand where to invest your resources and how to manage your Titans in the Dungeon.

For beginners, the most important principles are straightforward:

  • Prioritize Water Titans for your Dungeon-focused strategy.
  • Gradually develop Earth and Fire Titans for their elemental battles.
  • Understand how Elemental Doors and Neutral Doors work.
  • Invest in your main Titan tank instead of spreading resources too thinly.
  • Play manually when possible and manage your Titans' health carefully.
  • Develop your Water Totem and use Neutral Doors strategically.
  • Build a consistent routine and gradually work toward collecting 1,000 Titanite daily for your guild.

You don't need a perfect Titan roster from day one. What matters most is making smart investments and learning how to get more value from the Titans you already have.

With patience and a consistent strategy, you can build a stronger Titan team, improve your Dungeon progression, and contribute more to your guild.
