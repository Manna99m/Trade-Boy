Hero Wars Skin Upgrade Guide for Beginners: Which Skins Should You Upgrade First?

Skin Upgrade Guide By Trade Boy Updated: November 11, 2026 Estimated Read: 6 min

One of the biggest challenges for beginners in Hero Wars: Dominion Era is managing limited resources. There are so many heroes to upgrade, and choosing where to spend your resources can make a huge difference in your progress.

Hero skins are one of those upgrade systems where beginners often make expensive mistakes. Many players unlock a new skin simply because they like its appearance, without considering whether its stat bonus is actually useful for their hero.

⚠️ Don't Make This Expensive Mistake!

When you're playing as a free-to-play (F2P) player, every single Skin Stone matters! Spending 5,000 Skin Stones on the wrong stat can set your account progression back by weeks or even months.

In this guide, I'll explain how hero skins work, how to identify the most valuable skin bonuses, and which stats you should prioritize when upgrading your heroes.

We'll also look at key hero case studies—including Orion, Sebastian, Dorian, and Dante—to understand why different heroes require entirely different upgrade strategies.

1. Understanding How Hero Skins Work

Every hero in Hero Wars has multiple skins, and each skin provides a bonus to a specific stat.

Depending on the skin, you can increase stats such as:

  • Physical Attack: Amplifies basic attacks and physical scaling skills.
  • Magic Attack: Amplifies magical abilities and magic-based damage.
  • Health: Increases maximum hit points, allowing heroes to survive burst hits.
  • Armor: Mitigates incoming physical damage from enemy attackers.
  • Magic Defense: Mitigates incoming magic damage and magical barrages.
  • Dodge: Grants a percentage chance to completely evade incoming physical attacks.
  • Magic Penetration: Ignores a flat portion of the target's Magic Defense.
  • Armor Penetration: Ignores a flat portion of the target's physical Armor.

💡 The Golden Rule of Skins: Stat Bonuses Permanently Stack!

All upgraded skin bonuses stack, regardless of which skin is currently equipped. This means you do not need to equip a particular skin to receive its stat bonus. Once you unlock and upgrade a skin, its bonus is permanently and passively added to the hero's total stat pool!

Equipping a skin in your inventory only changes your hero's cosmetic appearance on the battlefield.

So, don't worry about which skin looks cooler or more imposing. What matters is the exact numerical bonus it provides to your hero's active skills.

2. Don't Upgrade Skins Just Because They Look Good!

This is probably one of the most common mistakes beginners make.

You unlock a new skin, see that it looks amazing, and immediately start dumping precious Skin Stones to level it up.

Before investing your resources, always stop and ask yourself one simple question:

"Does this skin improve something that actually makes my hero stronger in their designated role?"

For example, if you're using a hero mainly as a primary damage dealer, investing in offensive stats (Attack or Penetration) will produce immediate returns by eliminating enemy frontlines faster.

On the other hand, if you're developing a frontline tank whose sole responsibility is to survive incoming damage, investing in Health, Armor, or Magic Defense is vastly more valuable than offensive stats.

The right upgrade always depends on the hero's role and the exact abilities you're relying on in battle. That's why you must understand your hero's skills before spending Skin Stones.

3. How to Find the Best Skin for Your Hero

One of the easiest ways to identify the right skin is by checking the hero's skill descriptions in-game.

Open your hero's skills tab and inspect each ability. You can usually see which stats influence its damage or effectiveness:

  • Physical Attack: Improves abilities that scale with Physical Attack coefficients.
  • Magic Attack: Improves abilities that scale with Magic Attack coefficients.
  • Dodge: Increases the chance of avoiding physical damage and triggering dodge-reliant mechanics.
  • Armor: Reduces incoming physical damage.
  • Magic Defense: Reduces incoming magic damage.
  • Penetration: Helps your attacks bypass the enemy's corresponding defensive resistances.

Crucial Insight: Not every skill depends on a hero's attack stats! Some abilities depend entirely on their skill level (such as Dorian's Vampirism Aura). Upgrading an unrelated attack stat gives zero benefit to that skill.

Let's examine four real hero case studies to see this logic in action.

4. Orion – Prioritize Magic Attack

Orion Magic Damage Dealer

Top Priority: Mechanical Skin (Magic Attack) → Magic Penetration Skin

Orion is a powerful magic damage dealer whose main strength comes from rapidly launching missiles via his first three abilities.

If you check his skill descriptions, you'll notice that the damage of every single rocket scales directly with Magic Attack.

This makes Magic Attack the single most important offensive stat for Orion.

Total Destruction Magic Attack Scaling

Fires 6 magic missiles at enemies with the highest health. Missile damage scales directly with Orion's Magic Attack.

Magnetic Field Magic Attack Scaling

Detonates an area explosion in the enemy backline dealing magic damage that scales with Magic Attack and slowing enemy movement.

Antimatter Blast Magic Attack Scaling

Launches a high-yield projectile at the nearest target, dealing magic damage scaled with Magic Attack and stunning the target for 4 seconds.

Full Charge Energy Generation Passive

Passive ability that charges Orion's energy meter upon every attack, enabling him to launch Total Destruction barrages back-to-back.

🎯 Orion's Recommended Upgrade Order:

  1. Mechanical Skin (Magic Attack): Maximizes missile damage per barrage.
  2. Magic Penetration Skin: Ensures your magic attacks pierce heavily armored enemy tanks and supports.

The main lesson here is simple: if a hero's primary abilities depend on Magic Attack to defeat opponents, maximizing Magic Attack provides immediate, exponential combat returns.

5. Sebastian – Physical Attack Is Important

Sebastian Debuff Shield & Buffer

Top Priority: Default / Physical Attack Skin → Health / Armor

Sebastian is another great example of why checking skill descriptions matters so much.

At first glance, beginners assume Sebastian is merely a utility support hero who cleanses debuffs via his ultimate, Ode to Serenity. However, several of his most devastating combat skills scale directly with Physical Attack:

Battle Song Physical Attack Scaling

Increases the Critical Hit chance of all allies with a critical stat for 5 seconds. The crit bonus formula scales directly with Sebastian's Physical Attack.

Striking Solo Physical Attack Scaling

Whenever an ally lands a critical strike, it triggers an explosion of bonus Pure Damage. This extra damage scales heavily with Sebastian's Physical Attack.

Public Idol Physical Attack Scaling

Sebastian enters combat with an initial energy head start. The bonus starting energy scales with his Physical Attack, letting him cast his ultimate faster.

Ode to Serenity Ultimate / Level Dependent

Removes all current debuffs from allies and deploys a protective barrier shielding against up to 15 subsequent debuff attempts.

💡 Why Upgrade Sebastian's Physical Attack?

By boosting Sebastian's Physical Attack, you are directly multiplying the critical burst damage of your primary damage dealers (such as Isaac, Keira, Daredevil, or Lara Croft) while accelerating his energy generation. Always prioritize his Physical Attack skin before spending resources on defensive bonuses.

6. Dorian – Don't Waste Skin Stones!

Dorian Vampirism Healer

Top Priority: Save Skin Stones! Upgrade Skill 4 (Initiation) Instead.

Dorian is one of the most interesting heroes in Hero Wars because his primary purpose is fundamentally different from traditional healers and damage dealers.

Most players bring Dorian into their team specifically for his fourth skill, Initiation, which provides a passive Vampirism Aura allowing nearby teammates to recover huge amounts of health whenever they deal damage.

🚫 The Big Dorian Trap:

Dorian's Vampirism Aura does NOT depend on his Magic Attack! It scales exclusively with its skill level. Upgrading his Magic Attack skin only improves the damage of his bats (Wings of Night), which is negligible compared to his aura.

Initiation (Skill 4) Skill Level Dependent Only!

Creates a passive Vampirism Aura around Dorian. Nearby allies heal for a percentage of damage dealt. Scales exclusively with skill level—zero benefit from Magic Attack skin!

Wings of Night (Skill 3) Magic Attack Scaling (Low Priority)

Releases a flock of bats that deal magic damage twice and restore Dorian's health. While it scales with Magic Attack, this damage is minimal and does not warrant skin stone investment.

Fountain of Blood (Skill 1) Debuff Mark

Marks the nearest enemy with a blood curse for 7 seconds. Allied heroes recover health whenever they inflict damage on the marked target.

Ancestors' Amulet (Skill 2) Health Transfer

Dorian sacrifices 20% of his current health to immediately restore a large amount of health to the allied hero with the lowest current HP.

When you are a beginner with limited Skin Stones, you do not need to spend Skin Stones on Dorian's Magic Attack skin.

Instead, focus your Skin Stones on heroes that benefit directly from stat upgrades. If Dorian dies too quickly in battle, you can eventually consider upgrading his Health or defensive stats, but your main damage dealers and tank should always come first.

7. Dante – Dodge Before Damage?

Dante Dodge & Physical DPS

Top Priority: Romantic Skin (Dodge) → Physical Attack Skin

Dante is an excellent example of why you shouldn't automatically prioritize offensive attack stats on every damage-oriented hero.

While Dante's abilities benefit from Physical Attack, he is primarily played for his incredible Dodge-based survivability and counter-utility.

Whenever Dante successfully dodges an attack, he launches an additional retaliatory spear at an opponent. Higher Dodge means more frequent counters and significantly higher effective health against physical teams.

Foresight (Skill 2) Physical Attack & Dodge Scaling

Increases Dodge for Dante and all allies for 5 seconds. The team Dodge bonus directly scales with Dante's Physical Attack, while keeping Dante elusive.

Retribution (Skill 3) Dodge-Triggered Counter

Every time Dante successfully dodges an attack, he throws a retaliatory spectral spear at the nearest enemy. Higher Dodge activates this passive much more frequently!

Instrument of Fate (Skill 1) Physical Attack Scaling

Launches spectral spears at all enemies, dealing physical damage, knocking them backward, and disrupting enemy formations.

Shackles of Weakness (Skill 4) Main Stat Debuff

Dante's spears decrease the target's main stat (Strength, Agility, or Intelligence) for 5 seconds, crippling enemy damage and defenses.

🎯 Dante's Recommended Upgrade Strategy:

Consider upgrading his Romantic Skin (Dodge) first to unlock his defensive engine. Once Dante can reliably dodge incoming physical attacks, invest in his Physical Attack skin.

Bonus Synergy: Dante's Foresight ability also grants a team-wide Dodge buff to all allies, and that buff scales with his Physical Attack. Thus, Physical Attack enhances both his damage and team protection once his core Dodge base is established.

8. General Skin Upgrade Strategy for Beginners

You don't need to memorize every single skin in the game. Once you understand how skills scale with different stats, choosing skin upgrades becomes straightforward.

⚔️ For Damage Dealers

Prioritize offensive stats that directly improve their primary scaling abilities. Focus on Physical Attack, Magic Attack, Armor Penetration, or Magic Penetration.

🛡️ For Tanks

Focus strictly on survivability. Health, Armor, and Magic Defense are essential because tanks need to endure heavy burst damage and protect the team.

✨ For Support Heroes

Check what makes their utility effective. If their buffs scale with attack stats (like Sebastian), invest in attack. If their skills depend only on skill level (like Dorian), save your stones!

💨 For Dodge-Based Heroes

Prioritize Dodge when evasion is a core requirement of their survivability or counterattack kit (like Dante or Aurora), followed by attack stats that scale their team utility.

Remember: these are practical guidelines, not rigid rules. Always check the hero's actual in-game skill formulas and role in your team composition.

9. My Biggest Advice: Focus on Your Main Team

This is arguably the most vital piece of advice for any beginner in Hero Wars:

🏆 Concentrate on Your Core 5 Heroes

You do not need to upgrade every hero and every skin at the same time. Choose your main team of five heroes and direct 100% of your Skin Stones into their most impactful stats first.

Understand what each hero contributes to your team, identify their most valuable skin bonuses, and max those out before spreading resources to secondary heroes.

A well-invested core team of five will consistently outperform a large roster of underdeveloped heroes with scattered, half-leveled skins.

You can always revisit other skins later when your main team is fully established and competitive.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

❓ Do skin bonuses stack in Hero Wars?
Yes! All upgraded skin bonuses stack permanently regardless of which skin is currently equipped. Equipping a skin only changes your hero's cosmetic appearance.
❓ Should I upgrade skins just because they look cool?
No. Skin upgrades should always be determined by the stat bonus provided. Spending scarce Skin Stones on a stat your hero does not scale with is an expensive beginner mistake.
❓ Which skin should I upgrade on Orion first?
Prioritize Orion's Mechanical Skin (Magic Attack) first because all his primary missile barrage abilities scale with Magic Attack. Afterwards, consider his Magic Penetration skin.
❓ Why is Physical Attack so important on Sebastian?
Sebastian's Battle Song (critical strike buff), Striking Solo (pure bonus damage on crits), and Public Idol (starting energy) all scale directly with Physical Attack.
❓ Does Dorian need a Magic Attack skin upgrade?
No! Dorian's core Vampirism Aura (Initiation) scales strictly with its skill level, not Magic Attack. Save your Skin Stones for your main damage dealers and tank.
❓ Should I upgrade Dante's Dodge skin or Physical Attack skin first?
If Dante is used for frontline dodge utility and survivability, upgrade his Romantic Skin (Dodge) first to trigger his counterattack spears. Then upgrade Physical Attack to boost his damage and Foresight ally dodge buff.
❓ What is the best overall Skin Stone strategy for F2P players?
Focus 100% of your Skin Stones on your core 5-hero team. Prioritize offensive stats for damage dealers, defensive survivability (Health, Armor) for tanks, and only invest in support skins if their abilities scale directly with stats.
