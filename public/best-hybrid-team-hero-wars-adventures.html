Best Hybrid Team for Adventures in Hero Wars – Physical, Magic & Pure Damage

Adventure Guide Hybrid Strategy By Trade Boy Updated: November 13, 2026 Est. Read: 7 min

Have you ever joined an Adventure in Hero Wars, only to discover that the remaining path has 100% damage resistance against your entire team?

This is a devastatingly common problem, especially for beginner and mid-level players.

Imagine joining an Adventure with two guildmates. Both of them successfully complete their paths, but when it's your turn, the remaining path has damage resistance that completely counters your team.

⚠️ The Adventure Wipeout Scenario

If you're using a mono-physical damage team and the remaining path has 100% Physical Damage Resistance, your attacks deal zero damage. The exact same wipeout happens to pure magic teams against 100% Magic Damage Resistance. One player's mistake or bad matchup can ruin the run for all three participants, preventing everyone from collecting valuable Adventure pet chests!

So, what's the solution?

Instead of depending on only one type of damage, you can build a hybrid team that combines physical and magic damage, with additional pure damage potential.

In this guide, I'll explain how this team works, why it solves Adventure resistance mechanics, and how you can also dominate with it in other game modes like Arena, Grand Arena, Guild Wars, and Clash of Worlds.

Understanding Damage Resistance in Adventures

Before building a hybrid team, it's important to understand the different types of damage resistance you encounter in Dominion Era Adventures.

There are three main types of damage resistance:

  1. Physical Damage Resistance: Negates incoming damage from basic physical attacks, physical skills, and physical critical strikes.
  2. Magic Damage Resistance: Blocks magical abilities and spell damage completely.
  3. Pure Damage Resistance: Shuts down true/pure damage that normally ignores armor and magic defense.

The resistance percentage depends on the Adventure difficulty level and active map buffs. At higher Adventure levels (such as Adventure 11, 12, and 13), you frequently encounter paths featuring 100% complete immunity to a specific damage archetype.

The Solo Trap: When you join an Adventure where two guildmates have already finished their segments, you cannot choose which path is left. If you only have one damage type, you are gambling your run on luck. A hybrid team eliminates this gamble entirely.

The Solution: Build a Hybrid Team

A true hybrid team combines different damage sources while maintaining elite combat synergy. Simply placing random heroes together doesn't work—heroes must amplify each other's offensive outputs, cover defensive weaknesses, and sustain through extended waves.

This team combines relentless physical burst, explosive magical missile bombardments, pure damage piercing, adjacent stat amplification, and immortal vampirism healing. Let's examine each hero individually.

1. Dante – Main Physical Damage Dealer & Dodge Support

Dante Physical Damage & Dodge

Primary Function: Primary physical burst, frontline enemy knockback, and whole-team dodge defense.

Dante serves as our primary physical anchor. His ultimate launches spectral spears at enemies, inflicting heavy physical damage while pushing enemy frontlines backward into a tightly packed clump.

However, Dante offers much more than just raw damage. His dodge mechanics dramatically increase the survivability of the entire squad. Whenever Dante or teammates dodge incoming attacks, Dante retaliates with automatic counter-attack spears.

Instrument of Fate Physical Burst & Knockback

Fires spectral spears dealing massive physical damage to all enemies in path and pushing them back, clumping them up for Orion and Augustus.

Foresight Team Dodge Multiplier

Grants a huge dodge bonus to all allies for 5 seconds, shielding the team from lethal physical attacks.

Retribution Dodge Counter Passive

Whenever Dante dodges attacks, he retaliates immediately by hurling a spectral spear at the opponent.

Shackles of Weakness Main Stat Reduction

Temporarily reduces the main stat (Strength, Agility, or Intelligence) of targeted enemies, weakening their damage and armor.

2. Nebula – Damage Amplification & Support

Nebula Damage Buffer & Purifier

Primary Function: Massive dual Physical & Magic Attack transfer to adjacent allies, targeted healing, and debuff cleansing.

Nebula is the core engine that makes this hybrid composition hyper-lethal. Unlike buffers who only enhance one stat archetype, Nebula's passive transfers both Physical Attack and Magic Attack to adjacent teammates!

In this team positioning, Dante receives astronomical Physical Attack amplification, while Orion and Augustus receive surging Magic Attack bonuses. In addition, Nebula cleanses dangerous crowd-control debuffs and restores health to wounded allies.

Equilibrium Dual Stat Transfer Passive

Passively increases the Physical Attack and Magic Attack of adjacent allies by a colossal percentage of Nebula's own stats.

Serenity Healing & Cleanse

Periodically restores health to the most wounded nearby ally and cleanses them of negative status effects and debuffs.

Astral Projection Energy Drain Burst

Launches an orb into the enemy ranks that detonates upon activation, dealing physical damage and burning enemy energy.

Disharmony Targeted Physical Strike

Strikes the opponent with the lowest remaining health, dealing high single-target physical damage to secure quick eliminations.

3. Orion – Main Magic Damage Dealer

Orion Continuous Magic Missiles

Primary Function: Rapid-fire magic damage artillery, enemy energy acceleration, and backline stun control.

Orion provides nonstop magical missile barrages. Thanks to his 4th skill, Full Charge, Orion gains bonus energy with every basic attack, allowing him to launch his ultimate, Total Destruction, multiple times in rapid succession.

When powered by Nebula's Magic Attack boost, each individual missile strikes like a freight train. Whenever you encounter paths with 100% Physical Resistance, Orion completely dismantles the enemy frontline and backline.

Total Destruction Rapid Magic Missiles

Launches 6 armor-piercing magic missiles targeting the highest-health opponents. Scales aggressively with Magic Attack.

Magnetic Field Area Magic & Slow

Detonates an electric area blast in the enemy backline, slowing down enemy action speed and dealing magic damage.

Antimatter Blast 4-Second Hard Stun

Fires a concentrated energy sphere at the nearest target, stunning them for 4 uninterrupted seconds.

Full Charge Energy Generation Passive

Passively supercharges Orion's energy meter upon every attack, enabling him to cast Total Destruction every few seconds.

4. Augustus – Magic Damage & Pure Damage Potential

Augustus Magic & Pure Damage Converter

Primary Function: Targets low Magic Defense enemies, reduces enemy resistances, and converts excess penetration into devastating Pure Damage!

Augustus is the secret weapon that elevates this team from a standard hybrid into an unstoppable force. His abilities consistently seek out targets with the weakest Magic Defense.

His fourth skill, Superconductivity, reduces enemy Magic Defense with every hit. When his magic attacks completely overpower the enemy's remaining Magic Defense, the excess damage converts directly into Pure Damage!

Superconductivity Pure Damage Conversion

Reduces enemy Magic Defense. When magical attacks fully penetrate enemy resistances, damage converts into unstoppable Pure Damage!

Lightning Rod Targeted Lightning Strike

Summons lightning strikes targeting enemies with the lowest Magic Defense, neutralizing squishy supports and damage dealers.

Path of Least Resistance Chain Electricity

Arcs electrical damage across enemy ranks, softening up tanks and middle-line defenders.

Grounded Electromagnetic Shield

Provides defensive grounding that mitigates incoming burst and stabilizes Augustus against enemy magical counters.

5. Dorian – Healing & Team Sustain

Dorian Vampirism Aura Sustain

Primary Function: Permanent Vampirism Aura that allows rapid-firing allies to recover full HP while dealing damage, even after Dorian falls!

A multi-wave Adventure battle requires relentless sustain. Traditional healers must build up energy before healing, leaving teams vulnerable to early burst. Dorian solves this through his passive 4th skill, Initiation.

Allies stationed within Dorian's aura (Orion and Augustus) heal for a huge percentage of all damage they deal. Because Orion fires dozens of rockets and Augustus unleashes lightning, both heroes remain at 100% health constantly!

Initiation (Skill 4) Permanent Vampirism Aura

Surrounds nearby allies with a passive Vampirism Aura. Allies heal for huge HP chunks on every hit. Persists even if Dorian is defeated!

Wings of Night Bat Swarm Sustain

Releases a flock of bats dealing magic damage twice and restoring Dorian's personal health pool.

Fountain of Blood Blood Curse Mark

Marks an enemy with a blood curse; all allied heroes recover health whenever they strike the marked target.

Ancestors' Amulet Emergency Life Transfer

Channels a portion of Dorian's current health directly to the most severely injured ally on the battlefield.

Best Pet for the Team: Khorus

Khorus Control Immunity Pet

Top Priority Team Pet: Protects Orion, Augustus, and Dante from crippling control debuffs, silences, and stuns.

In Adventure mode, enemy nodes often feature heavy crowd control, silencing debuffs, and sudden stuns that can disrupt Orion's rocket channeling or prevent Dante from dodging.

Khorus is the ideal team pet for this lineup. Khorus absorbs incoming magical control spells and converts enemy status ailments into protection, ensuring your damage dealers cast their ultimate abilities without hesitation.

Why This Team Works Against Different Adventure Paths

Now let's examine how this hybrid powerhouse dismantles the most punishing damage resistance modifiers in Adventure mode:

🛡️ Situation 1: Enemy Has 100% Physical Damage Resistance

The Challenge: Dante and Nebula's basic physical attacks deal zero damage against the shielded targets.

The Solution: Orion and Augustus immediately step up as the primary damage dealers. With Nebula transferring huge Magic Attack to Orion and Augustus, Orion's rapid-fire missiles obliterate the enemy ranks. Furthermore, Augustus reduces enemy Magic Defense and converts penetrating damage into Pure Damage, wiping out the encounter effortlessly.

🔮 Situation 2: Enemy Has 100% Magic Damage Resistance

The Challenge: Orion's rockets and Augustus's lightning strikes are completely resisted by the enemy.

The Solution: Dante takes center stage. Boosted by Nebula's Equilibrium buff, Dante's Instrument of Fate spears hit with extreme physical force. Dante knocks the opponents backward while his high dodge triggers rapid Retribution counter-spears, cutting down the magic-immune enemies with pure physical damage.

⚡ Situation 3: Enemy Has 100% Pure Damage Resistance

The Challenge: True/pure damage modifiers and conversion effects cannot inflict damage.

The Solution: Unlike mono-pure teams (such as Heidi or Maya compositions) that stall out completely, this team has dual physical and magical primary damage sources. Dante dishes out physical devastation while Orion rains magical rocket fire, effortlessly overcoming the pure damage immunity.

💡 Pro Tip for Adventure Clearing:

While this hybrid team overcomes single-element immunities with ease, always inspect the node before initiating combat! Look out for stacked double-resistance modifiers or energy-drain conditions so you can adjust your pet or manual skill timing accordingly.

Where Else Can You Use This Team?

Another major advantage of this combination is that your resource investment is not locked solely into Adventure mode. These heroes form an elite, versatile setup across multiple core game modes:

🏟️ Arena & Grand Arena

The combination of Dante's dodge survivability, Nebula's dual buffs, Orion's rapid burst, Augustus's pure conversion, and Dorian's aura makes this a terrifying offense team capable of punching well above its power rating.

🏰 Guild Wars

Guild War fortifications frequently feature anti-physical or anti-magic defensive fortresses. Having a hybrid team allows you to crack open stubborn multi-layered defenses without building a dedicated counter from scratch.

⚔️ Clash of Worlds (CoW)

In Clash of Worlds, versatility is key. When opponent defenses deploy tricky engineers or mixed tanks, this lineup's diverse damage profiles bypass defensive shields and secure decisive victories.

Is This Team Worth Investing In?

For beginner and free-to-play (F2P) players, spreading resources thinly across 15+ heroes is the fastest way to stunt account progression. That's why investing in heroes with cross-mode utility is paramount.

This hybrid team offers unmatched resource efficiency:

  • Dante: The premier dodge specialist and physical DPS used in Arena, Tower, and Campaign.
  • Nebula: The most versatile support in the game, fitting into almost any team composition.
  • Orion: Easily obtainable through the Tower Shop, making his soul stones fast to star up.
  • Augustus: Provides rare pure damage conversion that scales into late-game PvP.
  • Dorian: Requires minimal skin/glyph investment—his game-winning Vampirism Aura scales strictly with skill level!

Final Thoughts

Adventure damage resistance modifiers can turn daily dungeon progression into a frustrating chore if your account relies on a single damage type.

By assembling this hybrid setup—Dante, Nebula, Orion, Augustus, and Dorian with Khorus—you gain total freedom to conquer any path your guild leaves behind, whether it features 100% Physical, Magic, or Pure Damage Resistance.
