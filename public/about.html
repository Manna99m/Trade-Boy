About Us

Welcome to Trade Boy, your ultimate companion and strategy hub for the popular mobile and web game, Hero Wars.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to provide the Hero Wars community with the most accurate, up-to-date, and powerful tools available. Whether you are a brand new player trying to figure out which Titans to level up, or a veteran Guild Master optimizing your Guild War defense lines, Trade Boy is designed to give you the competitive edge.

What We Offer

Trade Boy is a comprehensive suite of utilities tailored specifically for the complex mechanics of Hero Wars. We offer:

  • Interactive Draft Battles: Practice and perfect your drafting strategies against opponents in simulated environments.
  • Advanced Team Builders: Analyze synergies, counters, and overall team power before committing your valuable in-game resources.
  • Comprehensive Tier Lists: Stay ahead of the meta with constantly updated rankings for Heroes and Titans.
  • Detailed Strategy Guides: Learn how to tackle the toughest event bosses, such as the Archdemon or OSH, with optimal team compositions.

The Story Behind Trade Boy

Trade Boy was created out of a passion for the deep strategic elements of Hero Wars. We realized that as the game grew more complex, players needed better tools outside of the game client to plan their moves. What started as a small personal project has evolved into a robust platform serving thousands of players.

Community Driven

We believe that the best tools are built through collaboration. We constantly listen to feedback from our users and top players across various servers to ensure our data is accurate and our tools meet the real needs of the community. If you have suggestions or find a new counter-meta team, we want to hear about it!

Thank you for using Trade Boy. May your drops be legendary and your guild wars victorious!
