About Trade Boy

Welcome to Trade Boy, your ultimate destination for everything Hero Wars!

Who We Are

Created by a passionate veteran of the game (tradeboy_yt), this website was born out of a desire to build a central hub where the community could access top-tier strategies, team synergies, and advanced tools completely free of charge.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to help you elevate your Hero Wars strategy. Whether you're trying to crush the Archdemon, dominate in the Arena, or master the complex mechanics of Mega Drafts, Trade Boy is here to provide the insights and simulators you need to build the ultimate team.

Join the Community

We are constantly expanding our guides and tools based on community feedback. Be sure to check out our YouTube channel for in-depth video guides, follow our Facebook page for updates, and join our Discord to talk strategy with fellow legends!
